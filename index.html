BUNNY HOP

Turn your phone sideways to play

Made by Olga Bessonova GitHub LinkedIn olgabessonova.com

How to Play:

run left and right

Space or jump

Hold the left or right side of the screen to run

Tap to jump. While running, tap with another finger.

Jump on ghosts to defeat them: +50

Collect carrots: +10 each

You have 3 lives. A ghost bite or a fall into a gap costs one.

Run to the end of the level to win!
